## What the timeline shows

The useful story is not “every early idea was right.” It is:

```text
repair devices
   ↓
learn systems under real constraints
   ↓
form unusual computational ideas
   ↓
make them executable
   ↓
build controls
   ↓
find dead mechanisms and nulls
   ↓
correct them
   ↓
split the surviving mechanisms into reusable public tools
   ↓
write manuals, teacher guides, study material, and reproducible archives
   ↓
document enough for another engineer to rebuild or reject them
```

That is the through-line of the portfolio.
