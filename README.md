## Flagship systems

### COSMOS

Local-first experimental AI runtime and engineering workspace combining persistent memory, dynamic state, local model serving, modular controller systems, sensory summaries, autonomous maintenance loops, evaluation tooling, and owner-controlled operation.

**Inspect:** [NavisWORLD/Cosmos](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/Cosmos)

### Davis Cosmic Synapse Theory (CST)

Research lineage that evolved from broad systems/simulation language into a more defensible computational question: can compact evolving internal state modulate attention, memory, routing, and learning in useful, measurable ways?

**Inspect:** [The Theory of CST](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/The-theory-of-CST)

### 12D Hebbian Transformer lineage

Dynamic-state transformer experiments focused on state affinity, learned gating, mechanism preflight, silent-failure detection, parameter efficiency, and controlled architecture comparisons.

**Inspect:** [12D Hebbian Transformer v4.2](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/The-Cosmic-Davis-12D-Hebbian-Transformer-ver.4.2)

### Reality Bridge / Alien Conductor

A browser-native adaptive music lineage that listens to human performance, estimates musical context, schedules realtime audio, and grows toward a modular local AI band with independent musicians and a conductor layer.

**Inspect:** [Local AI Band](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/-reality-bridge-alien-conductor-local-ai-band) · [Sound Fixed III](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/REALITY_BRIDGE_ALIEN_CONDUCTOR_III_SOUND_FIXED)

### Universe / Reality Bridge simulation

Interactive and deterministic simulation work spanning realtime 3D worlds, cinematic camera modes, procedural environments, Python/C++ architecture, seeded state, external-data bridges, and evidence ledgers.

**Inspect:** [Reality Bridge Universal Probe Engine](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/Reality-bridge-universal-probe-engine-sim-) · [Living Universe Sim](https://github.com/NavisWORLD/Cosmic-synapse-the-living-universe-sim-engine-)

## Evidence standard

The presentation can be loud. The evidence labels stay strict.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IMPLEMENTED** | Inspectable code or artifact exists. |
| **OBSERVED** | Captured runtime evidence shows execution or state. |
| **MEASURED** | A declared benchmark produced a metric. |
| **NULL** | The declared success condition was not supported. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | Falsifiable interpretation awaiting evidence. |
| **METAPHOR / MODEL** | Useful design language, not a literal biological or physical claim. |

This portfolio does **not** turn persistence into a consciousness claim, quantum provenance into quantum advantage, phone sensors into medical sensing, or random-process exploration into guaranteed prediction.
